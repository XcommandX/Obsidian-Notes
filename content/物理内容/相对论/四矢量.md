定义Four-velocity:

$$
V^i = \frac{dx^i}{d\tau}
$$

$$
V^i = \frac{dx^i}{d\tau} = \left( \frac{dt}{d\tau}, \frac{dx}{d\tau}, \frac{dy}{d\tau}, \frac{dz}{d\tau} \right) = \left( \gamma, \gamma v_x, \gamma v_y, \gamma v_z \right).
$$

有$$
\eta_{ij} V^i V^j = -1.
$$

令 $c \to \infty$,  $u = v + u'$(经典力学)

令 $u' = c$,  $u = c$

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
\gamma_{u'} \\[4pt]
\gamma_{u'} u'_x \\[4pt]
\gamma_{u'} u'_y \\[4pt]
\gamma_{u'} u'_z
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
\gamma & -\gamma\beta & 0 & 0 \\[4pt]
-\gamma\beta & \gamma & 0 & 0 \\[4pt]
0 & 0 & 1 & 0 \\[4pt]
0 & 0 & 0 & 1
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
\gamma_u \\[4pt]
\gamma_u u_x \\[4pt]
\gamma_u u_y \\[4pt]
\gamma_u u_z
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
u_x &= \frac{u'_x + \beta}{1 + \beta u'_x}, \\[6pt]
u_y &= \frac{u'_y}{\gamma\,(1 + \beta u'_x)}, \\[6pt]
u_z &= \frac{u'_z}{\gamma\,(1 + \beta u'_x)}.
\end{aligned}
$$

定义四动量为：
$$
P^i = m V^i
$$

依照四速度和[[固有时]]的定义:
$$V^i = \frac{dx^{i}}{d\tau}=\gamma \frac{dx^i}{dt}$$

如果仍希望保持 $P^i = M\frac{dx^i}{dt}$的形式，可以令
$$
M = \gamma m
$$
